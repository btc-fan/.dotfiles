## Question

Which single text states how long and in what shape the agent answers, and is the length measured?

Today 3 texts say it: the 60-word limit in the global rules (telegraph style was removed 2026-11-19: replies use STE), the i-have-adhd rules, and the
Ponytail closing line. They can ask for different things in one reply, and no hook reads the result. A Stop hook
can count the words of the last reply and write them to a log without a block.

Decide: the one source of the rule; the cases that may be longer (an audit, an explanation); if the word count
is logged; if a number in the log ever becomes a block.
