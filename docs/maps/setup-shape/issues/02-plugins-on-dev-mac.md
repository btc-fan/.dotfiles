## Question

Which of oh-my-claudecode, Ponytail and i-have-adhd stay on in each project, and in what form?

Options for oh-my-claudecode: on in each project as today; on per project as on the servers; on, with its hooks
silenced by the switches that exist. Facts for the choice: it starts 4 of the 7 processes of a Bash call and 5 of
the 7 at a Stop; it has 3 walls of its own (read budget, drift guard, context guard); it injects project memory
with no switch; it appends agent commands to `~/.bash_history`; its switches do not stop the process starts.

For Ponytail and i-have-adhd: they inject 2,859 and 6,989 characters at each session start, and Ponytail also
into each subagent. Decide if their text moves into the global rules (one source) or stays as plugins.
